MGo run Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Thank you for using MGo run, a modern, privacy-first running and fitness tracking application designed for Apple TV (tvOS) and iOS. MGo run helps you record your runs, track indoor and outdoor workouts, monitor exercise routines, and maintain daily fitness goals with ease on the big screen.

We believe your personal fitness records, workout logs, and health statistics belong solely to you. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict commitment to user privacy and explains what data MGo run handles — and more importantly, what it does not collect.

1. Overview & Local-First Philosophy

MGo run is designed as a standalone, offline-capable utility. All workout logs, running records, exercise statistics, and training routines are created, managed, and processed entirely on your local device. The developer does not operate or maintain any external servers, databases, or cloud backends to collect, store, or access your personal fitness data.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

MGo run does not collect, track, store, or transmit any of the following data:

  • Workout & Running Records: Your running sessions, workout logs, exercise duration, distance, pace, routines, or timestamps are never transmitted to developer servers.
  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, Apple IDs, or contact lists.
  • Identifiers & Telemetry: We do not gather device IDs, Advertising Identifiers (IDFA), analytics, or user behavior tracking data.
  • Location Data: The app does not access, collect, or track your GPS location or IP address logs on developer servers.

3. Data Storage & Apple iCloud Sync

Your fitness and workout data are stored securely:

  • Local Storage: By default, all running and workout data is kept within the app’s sandboxed local container on your Apple TV / iOS device.
  • iCloud Synchronization (Optional): If iCloud sync is enabled, your data is synchronized exclusively across your own Apple devices using Apple’s native CloudKit / iCloud framework. The transmission and storage are encrypted end-to-end by Apple. The developer has no access to your iCloud account or stored workout data.
  • User Control: You have full control to edit, clear, or permanently delete your workout history and running logs at any time from within the app.

4. No Third-Party Tracking or Ads

MGo run contains no third-party tracking frameworks, advertising SDKs, or analytics services. Your user experience is completely free from trackers, profiling, and third-party monetization.

5. Security & System Permissions

MGo run adheres strictly to Apple’s tvOS and iOS security and sandboxing guidelines:

  • The application does not request unnecessary device permissions (such as microphone, camera, or contacts).
  • Data stored within the app sandbox is isolated from other applications on the system.

6. Children’s Privacy

MGo run does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe and suitable for users of all ages.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect future features or regulatory updates. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or MGo run, please contact us at:

Email: support@mvision.app

Summary

MGo run is a private running and fitness tracking tool. It does not collect, track, monetize, or transmit your personal data or workout logs. Everything remains secure and private on your own device.
