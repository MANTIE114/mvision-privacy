MMuse Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Thank you for using MMuse, a modern, privacy-first memo and note-taking application designed for Apple TV (tvOS) and iOS. MMuse helps you organize personal notes, daily memos, to-do lists, and reminders with ease on the big screen.

We believe your personal thoughts, memos, and notes belong solely to you. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict commitment to user privacy and explains what data MMuse handles — and more importantly, what it does not collect.

1. Overview & Local-First Philosophy

MMuse is designed as a standalone, offline-capable utility. All notes, checklists, memo titles, and text contents are created, managed, and processed entirely on your local device. The developer does not operate or maintain any external servers, databases, or cloud backends to collect or access your notes.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

MMuse does not collect, track, store, or transmit any of the following data:

  • Note Contents & Memos: Your note texts, checklist items, categories, tags, or timestamps are never transmitted to developer servers.
  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, Apple IDs, or contact lists.
  • Identifiers & Telemetry: We do not gather device IDs, Advertising Identifiers (IDFA), analytics, or user behavior tracking data.
  • Location Data: The app does not access or track your GPS location or IP address logs.

3. Data Storage & Apple iCloud Sync

Your notes and memo data are stored securely:

  • Local Storage: By default, all memo data is kept within the app’s sandboxed local container on your Apple TV / iOS device.
  • iCloud Synchronization (Optional): If iCloud sync is enabled, your data is synchronized exclusively across your own Apple devices using Apple’s native CloudKit / iCloud framework. The transmission and storage are encrypted end-to-end by Apple. The developer has no access to your iCloud account or stored notes.
  • User Control: You have full control to edit, clear, or permanently delete your memos and notes at any time from within the app.

4. No Third-Party Tracking or Ads

MMuse contains no third-party tracking frameworks, advertising SDKs, or analytics services. Your user experience is completely free from trackers, profiling, and third-party monetization.

5. Security & System Permissions

MMuse adheres strictly to Apple’s tvOS and iOS security and sandboxing guidelines:

  • The application does not request unnecessary device permissions (such as microphone, camera, or contacts).
  • Data stored within the app sandbox is isolated from other applications on the system.

6. Children’s Privacy

MMuse does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe and suitable for users of all ages.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect future features or regulatory updates. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or MMuse, please contact us at:

Email: support@mvision.app

Summary

MMuse is a private memo and note-taking tool. It does not collect, track, monetize, or transmit your personal data or note contents. Everything remains secure and private on your own device.
